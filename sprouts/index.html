Sprouts

A classic pencil-and-paper game — now on screen!

📐 The Basics

Sprouts is a two-player game. Start with a few dots on the page. On your turn, draw a line connecting two dots (or one dot back to itself).

🎮 How to Play

  1. Click and drag from one dot to another.
  2. Draw any path you like (curved or straight).
  3. Release on a valid dot to make a connection.
  4. Click on any line to place a new dot.
  5. Lines cannot cross existing lines.

🏆 Winning

Each dot can have at most 3 connections. The last player who can make a valid move wins!
